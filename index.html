Gmail SMTP Setup

Configure Google App Password to enable automated email sending

💡 Why is an App Password needed?

Google requires a 16-character App Password to send emails automatically through your Gmail account with attachments without needing manual approval for each email.

How to get your App Password in 1 minute:

  1. Open your Google Account Security settings.
  2. Ensure 2-Step Verification is enabled.
  3. Search for "App passwords" in the search bar.
  4. Create a new password named "Aarambh Fest", copy the 16 characters, and paste it above!
